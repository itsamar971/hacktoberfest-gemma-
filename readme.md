# 🌸 Bloom

**The gamified life system for ambitious women.**

Bloom is an all-in-one personal growth app that brings habits, dreams, money, travel, and journaling into one calm, personal space.

---

## The Problem

Most productivity apps are built for corporate task tracking. For ambitious women juggling career, money, wellness, travel, and emotional health, they feel cold and punishing.

- **Sterile checklists:** life becomes an endless backlog, and one missed day breaks your streak.
- **Fragmented tools:** budgeting in one app, travel in another, notes in a third.
- **Intimidating finance:** dense spreadsheets and jargon that make people avoid money planning.
- **Blank diary pages:** journaling demands discipline and offers no guidance.
- **Privacy concerns:** personal dreams and reflections end up on ad-driven platforms.

## Why Bloom

| Problem | How Bloom solves it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Guilt-driven streaks | A living **Digital Garden**. Habits grow a 5x5 garden, and missed days slow growth instead of resetting it. |
| Scattered tools | One hub for habits, dreams, money, travel, and journaling. |
| Big goals feel impossible | **AI Vision Boards** break a dream into a 7-day plan of steps under 20 minutes each. |
| Finance feels intimidating | **She Stacks**, an arcade of 11 mini-games covering investing, taxes, budgeting, negotiation, and more. |
| Blank journal anxiety | An **AI journal companion** that reflects, asks questions, and encourages. |
| Privacy | **Local-first storage.** Dreams and journals stay on your device. Only travel pins you mark Public are shared. |

---

## Core Features

- **Digital Garden:** gentle, visual habit tracking.
- **Vision Board & Dreams:** AI breaks big goals into micro-steps.
- **She Stacks Arcade:** 11 financial literacy games, including Wealth Garden, Tax Waterfall, and Negotiation Lab.
- **3D Travel Map:** interactive globe, passport stamps, photo memories, and an opt-in community feed.
- **AI Journal & Mood Haven:** mood tracking and a supportive AI check-in.
- **Curated Reads & Audio:** 3-minute articles you can also listen to.
- **Home Screen Widgets:** to-do and travel widgets for Android and iOS.
- **Customization:** Winter Mode snow, color themes, and adjustable font size.

## Tech Stack

| Area | Framework / Service | Why we use it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| App framework | **Flutter** (Material 3) | One codebase for Android and iOS, with smooth custom animations. |
| State management | **Provider** (ChangeNotifier) | Simple, reliable app-wide state. |
| Local storage | **Shared Preferences** | Local-first storage keeps private data on the device and works offline. |
| Backend & database | **Supabase** (Postgres) | Profiles and public travel memories. |
| File storage | **Supabase Storage** | Travel photos delivered through a CDN. |
| Maps | **Mapbox Maps Flutter** | Interactive 3D globe with vector tiles. |
| AI | **Google Gemini / Gemma API** | Dream breakdown, journal companion, and insights. |
| Audio | **audio_service**, **audio_session** | Background playback and lock-screen controls. |
| Widgets | **home_widget** | Native Android and iOS home screen widgets. |
